## Terms and Definitions

### A

- **[ADDITION](https://blue-prince.fandom.com/wiki/Category:Additions "Category:Additions")**: Additions are rooms that are added to the **[draft pool](https://blue-prince.fandom.com/wiki/Drafting_Pool "Drafting Pool")**. Most additions are removed from the draft pool at the end of each day. However, some additions are **[permanent](https://blue-prince.fandom.com/wiki/Category:Permanent_Rooms "Category:Permanent Rooms")** such as those found in the [DRAFTING STUDIO](https://blue-prince.fandom.com/wiki/Drafting_Studio "Drafting Studio").
- **ADJOINING**: Rooms are considered **adjoined** if they are connected to each other via a doorway.
- **[ALLOWANCE](https://blue-prince.fandom.com/wiki/Allowance "Allowance")**: A set amount of **gold** granted at the beginning of each day. Your **allowance** is typically left in the ENTRANCE HALL each morning.
- **ARCHIVED FLOORPLAN**: Floorplans that are hidden from view after drafting [ARCHIVES](https://blue-prince.fandom.com/wiki/Archives "Archives") are considered 'ARCHIVED FLOORPLANS'.

### B

- **[BEDROOMS](https://blue-prince.fandom.com/wiki/Bedrooms "Bedrooms")**: One of the most common type of rooms. Bedrooms are typically used for resting and regaining [steps](https://blue-prince.fandom.com/wiki/Steps "Steps"), and are identifiable by the violet borders of their floorplans.
- **[BLACKPRINT](https://blue-prince.fandom.com/wiki/Category:Blackprints "Category:Blackprints")**: Ancient floorplans from eras long past, excavated from the depths of history. These floorplans lack the colored borders of their modern counterparts.
- **[BLESSING](https://blue-prince.fandom.com/wiki/Shrine_Blessings "Shrine Blessings")**: A blessing is a persistent benefit that lasts for several days, typically bestowed from [SHRINE](https://blue-prince.fandom.com/wiki/Shrine "Shrine") after an offering of **[gold](https://blue-prince.fandom.com/wiki/Gold "Gold")** has been made.

### C

- **[CALL IT A DAY](https://blue-prince.fandom.com/wiki/Call_it_a_Day "Call it a Day")**: You may *CALL IT A DAY* at any time. This will end the current day and reset the rooms on the [estate](https://blue-prince.fandom.com/wiki/Estate "Estate"), your inventory and your steps. When you run out of steps, you will be forced to *CALL IT A DAY*.
- **CHECKED ITEM**: If, on your estate, you have drafted a [COAT CHECK](https://blue-prince.fandom.com/wiki/Coat_Check "Coat Check"), you may use it to **check** an [item](https://blue-prince.fandom.com/wiki/Items "Items"). This will allow you to retrieve that item on any day you have a COAT CHECK on your estate.
- **COLOR**: The color of a floorplan will usually indicate the type of room:  
	**Voilet** for Bedrooms,  
	**Orange** for Hallways,  
	**Green** for Green Rooms,  
	**Yellow** for Shops,  
	**Red** for Red Rooms  
	and **Blue** for Blueprints (*default type*)
- **[CONTRAPTION](https://blue-prince.fandom.com/wiki/Contraptions "Contraptions")**: [WORKSHOP](https://blue-prince.fandom.com/wiki/Workshop "Workshop") is a useful room in which you can experiment trying out different combinations of [inventory items](https://blue-prince.fandom.com/wiki/Special_items "Special items") in an attempt to create a new and unique **contraption**.

### D

- **[DEAD END](https://blue-prince.fandom.com/wiki/Category:Dead_Ends "Category:Dead Ends")**: A dead end is any room lacking a second doorway. If a room ever has additional doorways it is not considered a dead end, even if those doorways are blocked.
- **[DIG](https://blue-prince.fandom.com/wiki/Dig_Spot "Dig Spot")**: Occasionally, you may find piles of **dirt** on the estate. These 'dig spots', often found in Green Rooms, can be dug up with the help of a [SHOVEL](https://blue-prince.fandom.com/wiki/Shovel "Shovel") and may contain hidden items.
- **[DIRECTORY](https://blue-prince.fandom.com/wiki/Room_Directory "Room Directory")**: The **DIRECTORY** contains detailed information for all rooms on the estate that you have drafted. You may access the Directory at any time from the SYSTEM MENU or by pressing **\[R\]**.
- **[DIRIGIBLOCKS](https://blue-prince.fandom.com/wiki/Dirigiblocks "Dirigiblocks")**: In the [COMMISSARY](https://blue-prince.fandom.com/wiki/Commissary "Commissary"), players may find an arcade cabinet for the popular game DIRIGBLOCKS. To the dismay of bored members of the [staff](https://blue-prince.fandom.com/wiki/Staff "Staff"), the cabinet has not been in working order for the last several years. (Although the game is not currently accessible, it is scheduled to be added in a future patch.)
- **DIRT**: Occasionally, you may find piles of **dirt** on the estate. These 'dig spots', often found in Green Rooms, can be dug up with the help of a SHOVEL and may contain hidden items.
- **[DRAFTING](https://blue-prince.fandom.com/wiki/Drafting "Drafting")**: The action of selecting a floorplan from the three options presented to you after clicking a door is know as **drafting**.
- **[DRAFTING (TYPE)](https://blue-prince.fandom.com/wiki/Category:Drafting_Effects "Category:Drafting Effects")**: Certain rooms can affect the drafting pool and the drafting process. This type of room is fittingly known as a **"DRAFTING ROOM"** and are noted on their floorplans by the compass symbol.
- **[DRAFT POOL](https://blue-prince.fandom.com/wiki/Drafting_Pool "Drafting Pool")**: The **draft pool** is the total collection of currently available floorplans, from which three are **drawn** at the beginning of each draft.
- **DRAW**: **Drawing** is the initial pulling of three floorplans from the **draft pool** at the beginning of drafting. To illustrate the difference between **drawing** and **drafting**, remember that "Three floorplans are drawn, but only *one* is drafted."

### E

- **EAST WING**: The **East Wing** rooms of the house are the nine rooms that border the very right edge of the house.
- **[ENTRY](https://blue-prince.fandom.com/wiki/Category:Entry_Effects "Category:Entry Effects")**: Certain rooms have an effect that is only triggered upon physically entering the room. These rooms are marked on their floorplans by an icon of an open door.
- **[ESTATE](https://blue-prince.fandom.com/wiki/Mount_Holly_Estate "Mount Holly Estate")**: The Estate refers to all the explorable areas of Mt. Holly including the rooms currently drafted, as well as [the grounds](https://blue-prince.fandom.com/wiki/The_Grounds "The Grounds"). [The House](https://blue-prince.fandom.com/wiki/The_House "The House"), on the other hand, is only referring to the current rooms, as shown on the Mt. Holly Blueprint.
- **EXHAUST**: When your steps reach zero, you are **exhausted** and too tired to continue exploring. At this point, you must **call it a day**.
- **[EXPERIMENT](https://blue-prince.fandom.com/wiki/Experiment "Experiment")**: From the terminal located in the [LABORATORY](https://blue-prince.fandom.com/wiki/Laboratory "Laboratory"), scientists and technicians can create **experimental house features** by selecting a "trigger" and and "effect". This experimental feature will last until the end of the day.

### F

- **FLOORPLAN**: The life of every room begins as a floorplan, a square paper sketch, drawn by an architect, from a pool of potential ideas- the draft pool.

### G

- **[GEMS](https://blue-prince.fandom.com/wiki/Gem "Gem")**: Gems are valuable resources used by Architects to draft **Special Floorplans**.
- **GLOSSARY OF TERMS**: A feature on the NETWORK that you are now perusing: A list of words and architectural jargon that is intended to make simple, the terms you will encounter on the estate. Entries marked??????????? will become gradually revealed as you progress.
- **[GOLD](https://blue-prince.fandom.com/wiki/Gold "Gold") (COINS)**: **Gold** is the currency by which you can purchase goods and services from **[SHOP](https://blue-prince.fandom.com/wiki/Category:Shops "Category:Shops")** rooms on the estate.
- **[GREEN ROOM](https://blue-prince.fandom.com/wiki/Category:Green_Rooms "Category:Green Rooms")**: Floorplans that have a green border are known as Green Rooms. These rooms are associated with outdoor areas and gardens, and are *naturally* synergistic with one another.
- **[GROUNDS](https://blue-prince.fandom.com/wiki/The_Grounds "The Grounds")**: The outdoor area of the **estate** immediately surrounding the house. The **grounds** include many areas of interest, including the [campsite](https://blue-prince.fandom.com/wiki/Campsite "Campsite"), [apple orchard](https://blue-prince.fandom.com/wiki/Apple_Orchard "Apple Orchard") and [west path](https://blue-prince.fandom.com/wiki/West_Path "West Path").

### H

- **[HALLWAY](https://blue-prince.fandom.com/wiki/Category:Hallways "Category:Hallways")**: Orange floorplans, known as Hallways, are rooms that often contain many doors. They provide architects the means to branch off into different paths, allowing for greater flexibility.
- **HOUSE**: The **house** refers to the 45-floorplan grid of Mount Holly. Only the rooms currently drafted are considered part of the **House**.
- **HISTORY**: As an architect, you may wish to view the previous layouts and room configurations from days past. This useful feature can be access from the SYSTEM MENU.

### I

- **INVENTORY**: The items you have collected on any given day can be viewed and inspected from the Blue Print Menu \[tab\].
- **ITEMS**: Items refer both to **[Special Items](https://blue-prince.fandom.com/wiki/Special_Items "Special Items")** *(unique items in your inventory)*, as well as **[Resource Items](https://blue-prince.fandom.com/wiki/Resource_Items "Resource Items")** such as keys, gems, gold etc. As per the rules laid forth in your [Great Uncle's will](https://blue-prince.fandom.com/wiki/Will_of_Herbert_S._Sinclair "Will of Herbert S. Sinclair"), objects found on the estate must be left behind whenever you call it a day.

### K

- **[KEYS](https://blue-prince.fandom.com/wiki/Key "Key")**: **Keys** are a common resource used to open up locked doors on the estate. There are also some unique **[Special Keys](https://blue-prince.fandom.com/wiki/Special_Keys "Special Keys")** that can unlock special doors or rooms that only appear in specific locations in the house.
- **[KEYCARD DOOR](https://blue-prince.fandom.com/wiki/Security_Door "Security Door")**: Electric powered doors of reinforced steel that can only be accessed via **[Keycard](https://blue-prince.fandom.com/wiki/Keycard "Keycard")** are considered *Security Locked*. These doors appear with more frequency the higher up in **[Rank](https://blue-prince.fandom.com/wiki/Ranks "Ranks")** you are in the house.

### L

- **LOCKED**: As you explore the house you will find that some doors are locked. To continue exploring, you will need to use a key to unlock them. Locked doors appear more often the higher in **rank** you are in the house.
- **[LUCK](https://blue-prince.fandom.com/wiki/Luck "Luck")**: We all need a bit of it... **Luck** determines the likelihood that you will find additional items in rooms on your estate. Luck can be raised by certain items and rooms, marked on their floorplan by a clover.

### M

- **[MECHANICAL](https://blue-prince.fandom.com/wiki/Category:Mechanical_Effects "Category:Mechanical Effects")**: These rooms are often heavy in mechanics and often contain control systems that can interact with other elements on the estate. These rooms are marked by the symbol of a gear.

### N

- **[NETWORK](https://blue-prince.fandom.com/wiki/Network "Network")**: Each **terminal** on the estate is connected to a local network named "BLACKBRIDGE." This network allows users to remotely connect to other active terminals, as well as providing access to to staff services and other handy features, like this glossary!

### O

- **[OUTER ROOM](https://blue-prince.fandom.com/wiki/Outer_Room "Outer Room")**: Rooms draftable only from a door located outside of the house, on the grounds of Mt. Holly. West Path access is required before these rooms are accessible.

### P

- **[PERMANENT](https://blue-prince.fandom.com/wiki/Category:Permanent "Category:Permanent")**: Most things on the estate reset from one day to the next. It's the nature of the house. However, a few things are **permanent** and are usually marked as such. Permanent changes do not reset each day.
- **POWER SOURCE**: The estate's [BOILER ROOM](https://blue-prince.fandom.com/wiki/Boiler_Room "Boiler Room") is known as a POWER SOURCE because it provides steam power to various rooms in the house. Steam power is conveyed room-to-room via ducts that run across the ceiling, and power can be transported across great distances as long as you continue to string together [rooms that contain power ducts](https://blue-prince.fandom.com/wiki/Category:Contains_steam_duct "Category:Contains steam duct").
- **[PUZZLE](https://blue-prince.fandom.com/wiki/Category:Puzzle_Effects "Category:Puzzle Effects")**: Rooms 5, 6, 7 and 8 are all **puzzle rooms**. Which is interesting, because that's the same number of letters found in the [GALLERY](https://blue-prince.fandom.com/wiki/Gallery "Gallery"). Very curious...

### R

- **[RANK](https://blue-prince.fandom.com/wiki/Ranks "Ranks")**: Each row of rooms in your house make up a **rank**. As you progress north, the rank increases. There are nine ranks listed on Mount Holly's official blueprint. However, among the staff, whispers of a tenth Rank can often be overheard.
- **[RARITY](https://blue-prince.fandom.com/wiki/Rarity "Rarity")**: How often a floorplan is drawn from the drafting pool is determined by its **rarity**. There are four rarities: ● [Commonplace](https://blue-prince.fandom.com/wiki/Category:Commonplace "Category:Commonplace"), ●● [Normal](https://blue-prince.fandom.com/wiki/Category:Standard "Category:Standard"), ●●● [Uncommon](https://blue-prince.fandom.com/wiki/Category:Unusual "Category:Unusual"), and ●●●● [Rare](https://blue-prince.fandom.com/wiki/Category:Rare "Category:Rare").
- **REDRAW**: Some rooms and items allow you to throw back the first three options drawn from the drafting pool for a fresh set of potential rooms. This is known as **drawing new floorplans**.
- **[RED ROOM](https://blue-prince.fandom.com/wiki/Category:Red_Rooms "Category:Red Rooms")**: Beware the rooms of red! They offer powerful expansion options but come at a dangerous cost.
- **RESET**: Each morning, the house returns to its default position and all drafted rooms, inventory and resources are reset. Permanent changes, however, do not reset.

### S

- **[SANCTUM](https://blue-prince.fandom.com/wiki/Inner_Sanctum "Inner Sanctum")**: Mt. Holly's Inner Sanctum, one of the oldest rooms in the house, is a chamber deep underground shrouded in mystery and adorned by eight doors.
- **SECURITY LOCKED**: Electric powered doors of reinforced steel that can only be access via **Keycard** are considered *Security Locked.* These doors appear with more frequency the more higher up in **Rank** you are in the house.
- **[SHOP](https://blue-prince.fandom.com/wiki/Category:Shops "Category:Shops")**: Floorplans adorned by borders of golden yellow are Shops that typically offer goods or a service in exchange for **coins**.
- **SIGIL**: **Sigils** are ancient runes imbued with meaning that use symbology to represent the different aspects of the old realms. The book *[REALM & RUNE](https://blue-prince.fandom.com/wiki/Realm_%26_Rune "Realm & Rune")* serves as a helpful guide for those looking to decipher these [Ajeran](https://blue-prince.fandom.com/wiki/Ajera "Ajera") symbols.
- **SPECIAL FLOORPLAN**: These floorplans are slightly more rare and cost gems to draft. They often offer more unique, complex and exciting effects.
- **[SPECIAL ITEM](https://blue-prince.fandom.com/wiki/Special_items "Special items")**: A special item is referring to an item that is unique and inventory bound, as opposed to **resource items** such as keys, gems or coins.
- **[SPREAD](https://blue-prince.fandom.com/wiki/Category:Spread_Effects "Category:Spread Effects")**: When a spread occurs, items are randomly distributed across all rooms currently active on the estate. The more rooms in your house, the larger the spread.
- **[STARS](https://blue-prince.fandom.com/wiki/Star "Star")**: There are several ways to increase the amount of **stars** visible in the night sky. The easiest method, of course, is drafting [OBSERVATORIES](https://blue-prince.fandom.com/wiki/Observatory "Observatory"). Each additional star creates new formations of constellations, which in turn provide beneficial effects.

### T

- **[TERMINAL](https://blue-prince.fandom.com/wiki/Terminals "Terminals")**: Mt. Holly is equipped with several state of the art computer **terminal** stations that control various systems on the estate electronically. Each terminal is also connected to a central local **network**.
- **[TOMORROW](https://blue-prince.fandom.com/wiki/Category:Tomorrow_Effects "Category:Tomorrow Effects")**: Rooms that provide benefits in the future belong to a special category of floorplans: **TOMORROW ROOMS**. They are marked with a clock symbol.
- **[TROPHY](https://blue-prince.fandom.com/wiki/Trophies "Trophies")**: A brass distinction of genuine achievement, proudly displayed for all to see in Mt. Holly's very own TROPHY ROOM.
- **[TRUNK](https://blue-prince.fandom.com/wiki/Locked_Trunk "Locked Trunk")**: A small wooden chest often found in rooms, always locked, and usually containing a few items of interest.

### U

- **[UNDERGROUND](https://blue-prince.fandom.com/wiki/Underground "Underground")**: Resting far beneath the estate's foundations are a series of covert chambers and caverns, their existence known only to a few select individuals within the household.
- **[UPGRADE](https://blue-prince.fandom.com/wiki/Upgrade_Disk "Upgrade Disk")**: There are a number of data disks that can be found on the estate that contain potential room upgrades that were planned but never executed by [the Baron](https://blue-prince.fandom.com/wiki/Herbert_Sinclair "Herbert Sinclair").

### W

- **[WEST PATH](https://blue-prince.fandom.com/wiki/West_Path "West Path")**: A charming path that winds through the wooded area that runs alongside the west wing of the house. This path can be accessed from both the [garage](https://blue-prince.fandom.com/wiki/Garage "Garage") and the [west gate](https://blue-prince.fandom.com/wiki/West_Gate_Path "West Gate Path"), which is usually kept latched and locked.
- **WEST WING**: The West Wing rooms of the house are the nine rooms that border the very left edge of the house.
